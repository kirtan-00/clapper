Questions you'd actually ask

I run out of credits mid-shoot. What happens on the day?

Nothing on set. Logging takes, tagging GOLD, and CSV export never stop, paid or not. You just can't unlock a new project's PDF or Premiere export until you buy or renew a credit. Wrap the day, deal with it later.

Do credits expire?

No. Buy one today, spend it in eight months, it still works. There's no clock on a credit sitting unspent.

If I cancel Studio, do my unlocked projects lock again?

No. A credit spent on a project unlocks it for good, whether that credit came from a one-time buy or a subscription renewal. Cancelling stops future renewals, not past unlocks.

Is the free plan really free forever?

Yes. Two projects, no time limit, no card on file. That grant is a one-time thing, not a monthly refill, so use it on real work rather than a test file.

Studio vs Studio Plus, in one line?

6 project credits or 20 podcast hours a month, against 20 credits or 60 hours. Studio Plus is for the crew doing fifteen to twenty jobs a month, not the one doing two.
