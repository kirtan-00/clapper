Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last updated: 23 July 2026  ·  Effective: 23 July 2026

The short version. Everything you log in Clapper — your shot lists, takes, clip numbers, scenes, camera notes, and exports — is stored on your own device, not on our servers. We only receive your email if you choose to sign in with Google, plus a small export counter and anonymous usage events. We don't sell your data, we don't run advertising trackers, and there is no account required to log takes or export a PDF.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Clapper ("Clapper", "we", "us") collects, why, who processes it, how long it is kept, and the rights you have over it. It applies to the Clapper website and web app at clapboard.duckdns.org.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Clapper is an independent software project operated by Kirtan Purohit, based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. For any privacy question, correction, or deletion request, contact purohit.krick@gmail.com. We act as the data controller for the limited account and usage data described below.

2. The most important point: your production data stays on your device

Clapper is offline-first. Your projects, scenes, shot lists, takes, clip numbers, camera setup notes, imported script PDFs, and generated exports (PDF, Premiere XML, Resolve XML, CSV) are saved locally in your browser (IndexedDB / local storage) on the phone or computer you use. This content is not uploaded to us and we cannot see it. If you clear your browser storage or uninstall the app, that data is gone — keep your own exports.

3. What we do collect, and why

We do not collect your name (beyond what Google returns with your email), phone number, location, contacts, payment details, or any advertising identifiers. We do not build advertising profiles and we do not use third-party ad or social trackers.

4. Who processes data on our behalf (sub-processors)

  • Google (Sign-in with Google) — authenticates you and provides your email address. Governed by Google's own privacy policy.
  • Supabase — our backend host, which stores your account email, your export counters, and anonymous usage events.
  • Cloudflare (Turnstile) — a privacy-friendly bot check that runs when a gated action starts, to stop automated abuse. It is not an advertising cookie.
  • Groq — an AI inference provider used only in Script Mode to extract scenes from the screenplay text you import. It processes that text to return the result and does not receive your account details.

We share the minimum necessary with each and do not sell personal data to anyone.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account email & export counters — kept while your account exists; deleted when you ask us to delete your account.
  • Anonymous usage events — automatically deleted after 90 days.
  • Rate-limiting records — automatically deleted within minutes.
  • On-device data — controlled entirely by you, on your device.

6. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, where applicable, the EU/UK GDPR, you may:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • Correct inaccurate data;
  • Have your account and associated data deleted;
  • Withdraw consent (e.g. by signing out and stopping use of gated features).

To delete your data: email purohit.krick@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, or with the subject "Clapper data deletion request." We will delete your account email, profile, and export counters. We will confirm within 30 days. On-device data is removed by clearing your browser storage or uninstalling the app.

7. Security

Data in transit is encrypted over HTTPS. Backend tables use row-level security so a signed-in user can only read their own row, and the authoritative counters are written only by trusted server code. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the data we hold deliberately small.

8. Children

Clapper is a professional tool intended for film and video crews and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. International transfers

Our sub-processors may process data on servers outside India. Where that happens, we rely on the provider's own safeguards and transfer only the limited data described above.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Cookies & Storage Notice

Clapper does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. It uses browser storage only to make the app work:

  • Local storage / IndexedDB — stores your projects and takes on your device so the app works offline. Strictly necessary; never sent to us.
  • Sign-in session — if you sign in with Google, a session token is stored locally so you stay signed in. Cleared when you sign out.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile — may set a short-lived token when a gated action runs, purely to confirm you are not a bot.

Because these are strictly necessary or user-initiated, Clapper does not show a cookie-consent banner. You can clear any of this at any time from your browser settings.
