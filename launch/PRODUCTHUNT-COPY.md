## 7. DO NOT SAY

Claims that are now false, risky, or invented given the actual free-tier caps. Anything on this list would get called out by a commenter within the hour.

- **"Free Premiere export" / "free XML export"** without a qualifier. It's 2 free exports, not unlimited. Say "free exports to start" or name the number, never leave it bare.
- **"Free forever"** applied to anything except take logging and backup/restore. Those two are actually unlimited and free forever; exports, script mode, and call sheet parsing are all capped.
- **"Unlimited exports" / "no limits" / "no paywall"**. There is a paywall, it's just fair and clearly staged. Don't pretend otherwise.
- **Any specific price or plan name.** Pricing isn't live. Say "paid plans coming," nothing more specific.
- **"AI-powered app"** as a blanket description. Only Script Mode and call sheet parsing use AI; the take-logging core doesn't. Naming AI as the headline feature overclaims and undersells the actual differentiator, which is the offline logging loop.
- **Mentioning or linking the GitHub repo or source code, anywhere.** Demo link only: clapper.in.
- **"Native app" or "download from the App Store."** It's a PWA. "No install required" is accurate; implying an app-store presence is not.
- **"Never lose a take" / "crash-proof."** The real claim is narrower and true: if the tab dies mid-take, that take is recovered. Don't round it up to a blanket data-loss guarantee.
- **The old dev URL, `clapboard.duckdns.org`, anywhere in the listing, comments, or gallery images.** Every link goes to clapper.in.
